02 · Colour

Black canvas, green voice, amber action.

Three colours do the work. Green is the brand, amber is the only colour that asks for a click, black is the room they stand in. The rest are derived tones for portal structure and are marked as such.

Derived for the portal

Not on the marketing site. Added so a dense interface has depth, states and feedback without inventing a new palette.

Contrast

Computed live from the tokens. AA for body text is 4.5:1, large text 3:1.

PairForeground on backgroundRatioGradeVerdict

The one departure from the site

The marketing site sets white text on green tabs and on amber buttons. Both pairs fail AA at every size. Inside the portal, text on a green or amber fill is set in Ink, which passes at better than 10:1. The shapes, the colours and the case stay exactly as the site has them. Only the ink changes.

Where each colour goes

  • Green: headline emphasis, links, ticks, frames, dividers, the mark, selected states, success.
  • Amber: buttons, section labels, focus rings, progress, warnings. Never body text.
  • White: headings and body on black.
  • Grey: the wordmark, partner logos, outlined numerals.
  • Red: validation and wrong attempts only.

06 · Voice and imagery

Confident, warm, to one person.

How the copy sounds and how the photographs look. Both carry into onboarding content.

Tone rules

  • Speak to one person: you, your goals, whenever it suits you.
  • Benefit first, detail second.
  • Sentence case headings. UPPERCASE for nav, buttons, tabs and labels only.
  • Australian English.
  • Lists use the circle-tick. Never plain bullets.
  • One exclamation mark per page, at most.

Always / never

  • Always ONYX, RECOVER by ONYX, members, coaches, community, 24/7, group fitness classes.
  • Never Onyx, Recover, customers, trainers, users, clients.
  • Examples from the site: "Not only a gym, but also a community." "Train whenever it suits your schedule."

Imagery

  • Real coaches and members in the gym. Black ONYX tees. Dark interiors with green accents.
  • Portraits: arms crossed, straight to camera, grey brick with a diagonal green stripe.
  • Heroes dimmed 60 to 70% black. Never bright, never stock.
  • In the portal, onboarding uses the client's own photos of the real space. Simulations sit on plain black.

Portal rules drawn from the brand

  • Canvas is black. Cards are Surface (#111) with a hairline, upgrading to a 2px green frame when active.
  • Audiences: Employee is green, Manager is amber, Owner is white. The colour marks the area, never the text.
  • Progress is amber, the same colour as the button that continues it.
  • Text on green or amber is Ink. Everything else the site does, the portal does.
  • Navigation mirrors the site: a top bar with the lockup and a thick hamburger, a full-screen black overlay menu, and a bottom tab bar on phones.
  • Favicon and app icon: the green mark on a black square.
