Add to your home screen

iPhone and iPad do not let a website install itself, so this takes two taps in Safari.

  1. Tap Share at the bottom of Safari — the square with an arrow coming out of it.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

It opens full screen with its own icon. Anything saved in Safari stays in Safari: the installed copy starts fresh, so favourite your tips again from there.
